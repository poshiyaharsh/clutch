# BRAND — Clutch Product Identity

## 1. Brand Concept
Clutch represents high performance, focus, and coming through when it matters most: delivering under tight deadlines, presenting working software, and climbing the real-time leaderboard.

## 2. Visual Mark & Identity
- **Mark**: Clean modern geometric emblem with a spark in violet-blue (`#5B4CF5`).
- **Wordmark**: Inter font, bold 700 weight, -0.02em tracking.
- **Icons**: Lucide vector sprite (`wwwroot/img/icons.svg`). No emoji used in product interfaces.

## 3. Brand Voice & Copy Rules
- **Tone**: Calm, precise, confident, sentence case.
- **Action-Oriented**: Button labels explicitly state what happens: "Create team", "Save scores", "Join squad", "Lock judging".
- **Errors**: Plain language explaining what went wrong and how to resolve it.
- **Zero Filler**: No slang, no buzzwords, no cartoonish copy.

## 4. Primary Visual Assets
- **Logo Mark**: SVG spark symbol embedded with white fill inside violet-blue square badge (`.cl-brand-logo`).
- **Accent**: `#5B4CF5` (violet-blue) used deliberately for focal points, live states, and primary actions.
- **Surfaces**: Crisp white cards on `#FAFAFB` background (light) / deep obsidian `#121218` on `#0B0B0F` (dark).
