# BRAND — Clutch

## Name
Clutch: the moment you come through when it counts. Fits a 24-hour build, a last-minute demo, and the live leaderboard.

## Mark
A lemon sticker tilted -6 degrees, a thick ink "C", and a bubblegum dot sitting in the opening. The dot is the clutch moment: the score that lands at the last second.
Files: `design/clutch-mark.svg` (icon, favicon), `design/clutch-wordmark.svg` (mark + name).

## Brand line (pick one, use it consistently)
- **Show up. Clutch it.** (recommended)
- Build it. Ship it. Get scored. (current hero headline, works as supporting line)
- Win the room.
- Your team. Your moment.

## Voice
Short, direct, a little cocky, never mean. Plain verbs, sentence case. "Create team", "Save scores", "You’re in."
Examples: empty leaderboard: "No scores yet. Judges are warming up." Success: "Scores saved." Closed event: "This one’s wrapped. See the final ranks."

## Usage rules
- Mark needs clear space equal to the dot's diameter on every side. Minimum size 24px.
- Use only theme colors: Lemon sticker, Ink outline, Bubblegum dot. On dark backgrounds keep the sticker as is.
- Do not rotate the mark further, outline it again, or put it on busy photos without a Paper-colored plate.
- Wordmark text is Bricolage Grotesque 800, tracking -0.03em.
